How the pressure score works

Method: win-probability model (out-of-fold) → leverage = expected |Δ win probability| on the next ball → Pressure Index. Batter value = win probability added; bowler value = its negative. Performance is compared with an average player in the same situation. Eligibility: 150 pressure balls and 15 pressure innings (bowlers 120 / 15), active in 2026.
